# A signature phrase is a deliberate, recurring device for that section. The checker enforces:
#   1. the phrase MUST appear in that section every time the section runs,
#   2. it is exempt from the repeated-phrase flags inside that section,
#   3. it must NOT appear in any other section.
# Only sections whose purpose earns a signature have one.
