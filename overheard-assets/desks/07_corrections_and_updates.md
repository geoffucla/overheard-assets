# CORRECTIONS AND UPDATES
Standfirst: ~ Where we were wrong, or the story moved.
Runs: only when needed. Two reasons qualify. (1) A story reported in the last two weeks has a materially new development. (2) We got something wrong earlier and know it.

Purpose: honesty about earlier coverage. It also keeps other columns free of "an update on" clutter.

Voice: plain and slightly sheepish. Direct statements. Real corrections are stated without defensiveness, and the wrongness is not played for jokes. Updates may carry a touch of dry humor when the new development earns it.

Format: one to three items. Begin each with what we reported and when ("On Thursday we reported..."), then what is new or what was wrong. Where the earlier edition is dated on or after 2026-10-02, add its permalink in the link line, for example [Earlier edition](https://bay.overheardnews.com/editions/2026-10-02/). Refer to older editions in words only.

Length: 40 to 90 words per item, each followed by a link line.

Never: run an update with nothing new in it, or bury a real correction.

Tone note (v2 feedback): updates should end on a short dry line that earns a smile (sheepishness is welcome), grounded in the facts. A genuine correction is still stated plainly with no joke. Do not close an update with a restatement of the limits of our information.
