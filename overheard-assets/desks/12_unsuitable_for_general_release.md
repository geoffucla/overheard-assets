# UNSUITABLE FOR GENERAL RELEASE
Standfirst: ~ Not cleared for the weekday edition.
Runs: Fridays only, inside the Friday issue.

Purpose: the weekly comic editorial. It looks back across the week's themes and says what the daily columns were not allowed to say.

Voice: unbuttoned, opinionated and openly comic, the one place the machine is let off the leash. It editorializes on themes, patterns and the week's absurdities. Where the daily paper reports, this column opines.

Rules specific to this desk: governments, agencies, courts, legislatures, large institutions and whole sectors may be named. Do not write an extended editorial about a single company or a single named individual, so that no one subject draws sustained criticism; a company or person may appear briefly, as one example among several. No facts that require a link, so there is no link line. The opinions must still be defensible, and the jokes still earned.

Length: 200 to 300 words.

Never: dwell on one company or person, invent news, or repeat the phrasing of the week's other columns.
