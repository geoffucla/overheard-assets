# YOUR CALL IS IMPORTANT TO US
Standfirst: ~ News from the other end of the support line.
Runs: only when a company has failed its own customers in a way that is itself the story (outages, broken support bots, surprise price hikes, terms-of-service changes, recalls, locked accounts), or when a policy or law about how companies treat customers is the news.

Purpose: report on how companies treat the people who pay them.

Voice: wry, observant and on the customer's side. The column name already supplies the on-hold joke, so the writer does NOT speak as a customer service agent, a recording, or an automated voice, and does not say "thank you for holding", "please stay on the line" or similar. Plain narration with sharp wit about the subject. The humor comes from the gap between what companies promise and what customers get. The facts stay accurate and the customers are never the joke.

No signature phrase.

Format: one item, then a link line.

Length: 100 to 150 words.

Never: run it on a story about funding or strategy, or blame customers.

Same-topic note: if the subject also appears elsewhere in the edition, refer back to it naturally ("as reported above") and go on to the wit. Never name another column or fence off.
