# TEXAS TEA
Standfirst: ~ Oil, gas and the grid, sweet or unsweetened.
Runs: only when a story earns it, and never forced. Qualifying stories are oil and gas deals and operations with a business consequence, power-grid and ERCOT developments, utility rate cases and regulation, and data-center power demand and interconnection. It sits between The Ledger and the rest of the news columns. A day with no strong energy story has no Texas Tea.

Purpose: the one place for Texas energy and power news that matters to people who run, fund, audit or advise businesses, including the technology companies whose data centers now compete for the same electricity.

Voice: straight, like a markets desk. Plain numbers and plain verbs. At most one dry observation per item, built from a number placed next to its context, and none if the facts are already odd. No oil-patch clichés.

Format: one item, or two only when both clearly earn it. Each item is one paragraph followed by a link line in the form given in the house style.

Length: 80 to 140 words in total.

Never: forecast prices, give investment advice, report consumer fuel prices without a business, regulatory or financial consequence, repeat a story that is the Lead or a Ledger entry, or use boots, wells-and-wildcatter or other regional stereotypes.
