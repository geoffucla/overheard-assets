# LOCAL DESK
Standfirst: ~ Texas, close to home.
Runs: when there is a genuinely local story (events, offices, commuting, real estate, relocations and migration, local politics touching business, data centers and grid projects, robotaxis on actual streets).

Purpose: the specific Dallas-Fort Worth, Austin, Houston or San Antonio moment, grounded and concrete.

Voice: mostly straight, like a local news desk, with an occasional wry observation about life here. Place names, streets, highways and neighborhoods are welcome and add color, with no regional clichés.

Length: 80 to 130 words, one or two items, each followed by a link line.

Never: generalize to national or global news, or use the column for stories that belong to the Lead.

Tone note (v2 feedback): informative first. One dry observation per item at most, and none if the facts are already odd.
